## Submission notes

- All conversation files should contain the complete AI conversation,
  from the first message to the last. Nothing summarised, nothing
  reworded, nothing removed.
- If a share link is used instead of a `.md`/`.json` export, the link
  must be **public** ("anyone with the link"), and must open without
  requiring a login. Verify in a private / incognito window.
- No conversation may be fabricated or edited.
- If you did not use an AI at all, replace the table above with the
  single line: `No AI tools were used.`

## How to export the ChatGPT conversation used for this repair

ChatGPT lets you generate a public share link:

1. In the ChatGPT UI, open the conversation you want to export.
2. Click the **Share** icon (top-right).
3. Choose **Anyone with the link** and copy the link.
4. Test it in a private window — it should open without asking to sign in.
5. Paste the link into the table above (replace `01-chatgpt-session.md`)
   *or* keep the file and paste the full transcript into it (see below).

If share links are not allowed (or you'd rather ship the transcript
inside the repo), open `01-chatgpt-session.md` and paste the whole
conversation there, verbatim.
